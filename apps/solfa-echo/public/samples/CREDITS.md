# Sample credits

One mp3 per MIDI note (`<sound>/<midi>.mp3`, E2 = 40 to A5 = 81), taken from the pre-rendered
soundfonts at https://github.com/gleitz/midi-js-soundfonts and trimmed to 1.6 s, normalized, faded out
and re-encoded as 56 kbps mono.

- `voice/`: `voice_oohs` from the Musyng Kite soundfont by Gold Musyng.
  Licensed CC BY-SA 3.0 (https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by-sa/3.0/); these trimmed files are
  shared under the same license.
- `piano/`: `acoustic_grand_piano` from the FluidR3_GM soundfont by Frank Wen.
  Licensed CC BY 3.0 (https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by/3.0/).
